# BURROW ZERO — Player PvP Silo Raid v0.1

**Status:** Direction baseline  
**Model:** Asynchronous PvP

---

# 1. Core Principle

A player's Silo is both:

- home base;
- dungeon another player may infiltrate.

PvP must use the same gameplay language as PvE.

No separate auto-battle mode.

---

# 2. Unlock

Prototype:

- PvP unlock at CCL8;
- player completes at least one NPC Silo Raid first;
- explicit permanent account opt-in is required after that NPC raid; CCL8 alone is not consent;
- first PvP attack offers Resource Raid or Intelligence Raid; Command Assault unlocks after one successful PvP extraction;
- opting in exposes the Silo and explicitly contested sites under account shields; private PvE sites stay private forever.

All Silo/site PvP eligibility is governed by PVP_MATCHMAKING_PROTECTION §4–8. CCL6–7 and non-opted-in accounts cannot be attacked. The player may remain PvE-only forever.

---

# 3. Attack Flow

```
FIND TARGET
→ SCOUT
→ CHOOSE RAID OBJECTIVE
→ PREPARE SQUAD
→ INFILTRATE
→ REVEAL ROOMS
→ COMPLETE / ABANDON
→ EXTRACT
→ RESOLVE REWARDS
```

---

# 4. Defender Representation

Defender is offline/asynchronous.

Their real:

- room layout;
- defense assignment;
- doors;
- turrets;
- security rooms;
- selected defense squad;
- power topology;

are converted into an immutable raid snapshot at accepted deployment. Later building, staffing, gear or power changes affect future snapshots only. Selected defenders are persistent exclusive combat assignments: they may continue base jobs, but cannot depart on expedition/outgoing PvP/site work until explicitly unassigned before an incoming lock. An account can attack while being attacked only with a disjoint unassigned squad and gear; incoming acceptance therefore never needs to recall an already deployed defender. The single account incoming lock prevents duplicate defense reservations. Settlement injury/fatigue applies once to the real survivor; snapshot death never becomes permanent defender death.

Defender cannot lose permanent building ownership.

---

# 5. Raid Objectives

### Resource Raid
Take exposed resources.

### Intelligence Raid
Recover system-generated Combat Data / scouting intel; never debit owned defender Data.

### Sabotage
Temporarily reduce room output.

### Blueprint Hunt
Reach protected research/armory objective for system-generated fragments; never steal owned blueprint/gear.

### Command Assault
High prestige, low raw loot.

Not every objective is available against every target.

---

# 6. Layout Reveal

Initial scout:

- entry;
- 1–3 known rooms;
- approximate Command direction;
- security estimate.

During raid:

- adjacent rooms reveal progressively;
- hidden connection may remain unknown;
- decoy/false reading can exist later.

No full Clash-style overhead reveal.

---

# 7. Alarm

Uses NPC Silo Alarm model.

Player defenses can modify:

- Alarm gain;
- reinforcement threshold;
- response squad;
- door lockdown.

---

# 8. Defense Squad

Defender assigns **up to5 survivors from the first PvP unlock at CCL8**. There is no separate3→4→5 PvP-defense progression. CCL6–7 security squads have cap4 for PvE defense only. Fewer assigned defenders are valid; no free replacement survivors are created. Guards already working at Resource Sites are unavailable for this Silo assignment.

They are AI-controlled.

Defense squad injury does not create permanent death from offline raid in baseline.

Reason:

asynchronous punishment must not destroy named survivor attachment while player sleeps.

Instead:

- temporary Injury/Fatigue;
- Clinic time;
- defense cooldown.

---

# 9. Permanent Death Rule in PvP

Attacker's deployed units:

- can die under normal expedition rules.

Defender offline units:

- cannot permanently die from normal async defense.

Special opt-in hardcore/event modes may change this later.

---

# 10. Exposed Resources

Only unreserved Scrap, Components and Chemicals in the attacked Silo stockpile are eligible. For each resource:

```
Exposure[r] = min(floor(10% * current unreserved Silo stock[r]),
                  remaining defender rolling24h cap[r])
```

The fixed CCL cap table and authoritative reserve→extract→commit/release transaction are in PVP_MATCHMAKING_PROTECTION §11. Reserve at accepted deployment; defender cannot spend reserved stock; successful extraction debits only what attacker carries, once. Failed extraction/abandon/timeout releases all theft reservations with zero resource loss. One incoming lock covers the defender account, including every site; concurrent Silo/site attacks cannot reserve duplicate budgets.

Food, Water, Supply, all strategic materials/Data, Credits, gear, progression items and blueprint ownership are non-stealable. Silo attacks never reach Resource Site local buffers or Return Inbox overflow. Resource objectives are unavailable when exposure0; other objectives still obey shield/consent rules. System rewards do not count as defender loss and cannot be generated by repeating settlement.

Example: CCL8 stock1,000 Scrap, committed loss40 in last24h → exposure20 under cap60. Extract12 → debit12/ledger52; fail → debit0/ledger40. Midnight does not reset either ledger.

---

# 11. Defender Loss

Defender may suffer:

- small resource loss;
- room temporary damage;
- defense squad Injury/Fatigue;
- short production disruption;
- rank change.

Never:

- lose room permanently;
- lose CCL;
- lose unique survivor;
- lose unique blueprint ownership.

---

# 12. Attacker Risk

Attacker risks:

- Supply;
- consumables;
- field loot;
- unit injury/death;
- failed raid charge;
- rank.

Attacker cannot endlessly probe without cost.

---

# 13. Retreat

Attacker may extract early.

This preserves PvE philosophy:

> knowing when to leave is skill.

Early extract:
- commits carried eligible raid loot only on successful extraction under the shared transaction;
- yields less prestige;
- avoids deeper defense.

The active attack deadline is15 real minutes from departure, including outbound travel and suspension. At timeout, automatically accept extraction only if the squad is currently at a cleared valid exit and satisfies normal escort/cargo requirements; otherwise failed-extraction rules settle the raid and release reservations. Return travel may complete later. Accepted extraction commits loot/defender consequences immediately and idempotently; subsequent return travel cannot revoke or duplicate those committed rewards. No new room/loot/combat action is allowed after extraction acceptance.

---

# 14. Command Core

Reaching Command Core does not "destroy the base".

Reward:

- prestige;
- Combat Data;
- Encrypted Combat Cache;
- seasonal score;
- temporary defender debuff small/limited.

No total destruction mechanic.

---

# 15. Sabotage Limits

Possible temporary effects:

- Workshop -10% speed for 30 min;
- Security offline 15 min;
- Radio scan penalty 20 min;
- Resource Site link interrupted15min (collection delayed; no buffer deletion).

Sabotage effects apply only when its objective completes AND attacker extracts successfully. Repeated same effects keep the later expiry, never add durations or multiply penalties. No effect lasts longer than30min in this baseline. On failure, release the objective without applying the sabotage debuff; ordinary defender combat injuries still follow defense settlement.

No overnight account ruin.

---

# 16. Defense Design

Player can influence raid path via:

- room placement;
- doors;
- security;
- power;
- traps;
- response squad;
- hidden route.

But must preserve mandatory path validity.

No impossible maze/exploit layout.

---

# 17. PvP Rewards

Main:

- Combat Data;
- Credits;
- Encrypted Combat Cache;
- rank/prestige;
- limited exposed resources.

PvP is not the only source of endgame power.

Most core power still has PvE route.

---

# 18. Anti-Grief

- matchmaking band;
- attack cooldown on same defender;
- protection after heavy loss;
- daily exposed-resource cap;
- no permanent defender death;
- no CCL downranking;
- no zeroing base.

---

# 19. Success Criteria

PvP works if:

1. attacker feels they are exploring another player's real base;
2. defender layout matters;
3. loss is meaningful but not devastating;
4. retreat remains valid;
5. PvP gives prestige/alternate progression, not mandatory domination.


# 20. PvP Acceptance Predicates

- CCL8 unconsented player: cannot attack/be attacked even if Scout, Silo or site is visible.
- First opted-in CCL8 PvP: defense assignment allows5; no hidden mid/late unlock.
- Same defender Silo and site deployed against concurrently: exactly one accepted; rejected attempt has no Supply/charge/shield debit.
- Force-close after extraction acknowledgement: replay recorded reward/debit, never roll or debit again.
- Raid paused at14m59s: real clock continues; at15m extract automatically only if already at a cleared valid exit with valid escort/cargo; otherwise settle failure, release theft and preserve defender stock.
- Defender spends materials during raid: spending excludes reserved stock; successful extraction still has exactly the reserved backing.
- Revenge against shielded defender: rejected until shield expires; no bypass or automatic opt-in.

Expected tests for implementation, not runtime QA evidence.
