# BURROW ZERO — Faction System v0.1

**Status:** Direction baseline

---

# 1. Purpose

Factions provide:

- world identity;
- contracts;
- reputation;
- alternate technology;
- moral/strategic choices;
- regional conflict.

They are not just shops.

---

# 2. Initial Factions

## Continuity
Old-government / institutional survival.

Values:
- order;
- security;
- restoration.

Gameplay:
- military gear;
- defense;
- authorization tech.

## Free Settlements
Independent survivor communities.

Values:
- autonomy;
- trade;
- cooperation.

Gameplay:
- logistics;
- trade;
- recruitment;
- civilian tech.

## Earthborn
Surface-adapted survivors.

Values:
- adaptation;
- resilience;
- distrust of bunker elites.

Gameplay:
- hazard resistance;
- scouting;
- surface routes.

## Archivists
Knowledge preservation.

Values:
- research;
- historical data;
- controlled experimentation.

Gameplay:
- Research Data;
- Bestiary;
- blueprint efficiency.

## Ascendants
Pro-alien technology movement.

Values:
- transformation;
- alien integration.

Gameplay:
- Alien gear;
- Exposure trade-offs.

---

# 3. Reputation

Scale:

```
-100 → +100
```

Bands:

- Hostile: -100 to -51
- Distrusted: -50 to -21
- Neutral: -20 to +19
- Trusted: +20 to +49
- Allied: +50 to +79
- Exalted: +80 to +100

---

# 4. Reputation Sources

Gain:

- contracts;
- rescue;
- trade;
- boss help;
- return faction asset;
- regional event.

Lose:

- attack faction target;
- betray contract;
- support rival;
- steal special asset.

Not every decision changes reputation.

---

# 5. Rivalries

Prototype relations:

- Continuity ↔ Free Settlements: tension;
- Archivists ↔ Ascendants: strong tension;
- Earthborn ↔ Continuity: distrust.

Helping one faction may reduce another only on explicit conflict contract.

Avoid hidden reputation punishment.

---

# 6. Reputation Rewards

### Trusted
- better contracts;
- shop discount;
- faction intel.

### Allied
- faction blueprint;
- special survivor;
- region access;
- resource contract.

### Exalted
- cosmetic identity;
- unique sidegrade tech;
- story branch.

No faction gives strictly mandatory best-in-slot gear.

---

# 7. Faction Store

Uses Credits + faction token where appropriate.

Store offers:

- consumables;
- gear variants;
- cosmetics;
- blueprint fragments;
- utility.

Rotation should not force daily login.

Weekly or stable catalog preferred.

---

# 8. Faction Tech

Faction technology changes options.

Examples:

Continuity:
- reinforced security door.

Free Settlements:
- efficient logistics module.

Earthborn:
- hazard suit variant.

Archivists:
- improved analysis module.

Ascendants:
- alien neural interface with Exposure cost.

Sidegrades > raw superior power.

---

# 9. Contracts

Faction contracts:

- recover;
- rescue;
- investigate;
- clear;
- escort abstractly;
- sabotage rival;
- capture/hold;
- boss objective.

Contract shows faction consequence before accept.

---

# 10. Story Choice

Player can be friendly with multiple factions.

No forced permanent faction lock early.

Late story may require temporary alignment or choice.

---

# 11. Faction-Controlled Nodes

Faction can control:

- settlement;
- depot;
- lab;
- resource site;
- Silo.

Player options:

- trade;
- accept mission;
- negotiate access;
- raid if hostile.

---

# 12. Hostility

Hostile faction:

- patrol encounters;
- closed store;
- ambush chance;
- hostile target access.

Recovery possible through:
- reparations;
- neutral mediator;
- story mission.

Avoid irreversible accidental hostility.

---

# 13. Faction Survivor

Faction recruits are named survivors, not hero cards.

They may have:
- profession;
- unique trait;
- visual accessory;
- history.

No duplicate shard system.

---

# 14. Success Criteria

Faction system works if:

1. factions feel ideologically and mechanically distinct;
2. reputation changes content;
3. rewards are meaningful sidegrades;
4. choices are visible;
5. player is not forced into one faction immediately.
