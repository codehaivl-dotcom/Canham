# BURROW ZERO — Research & Technology Tree v0.1

**Status:** Prototype system baseline  
**Unlock:** Research Lab at CCL5

---

# 1. Purpose

Research converts exploration knowledge into permanent options.

Research should unlock:

- new systems;
- recipes;
- better efficiency;
- tactical sidegrades.

It should not be a long list of generic +2% stat nodes.

---

# 2. Research Currency

Main:

**Research Data**

Special requirements may include:

- Bestiary Sample;
- Military Data;
- Prototype Core;
- Alien Material;
- faction reputation.

No core research requires a PvP-only resource.

---

# 3. Research Time

Baseline bands:

| Tier | Time |
|---|---:|
| I | 5–15 min |
| II | 20–45 min |
| III | 1–2 h |
| IV | 3–6 h |
| V | 8–12 h |

Research Lab level modifies speed.

No multi-day mandatory research in baseline.

# 4. Branches

Canonical branches:

1. Survival
2. Expedition
3. Combat
4. Security
5. Engineering
6. Xenobiology
7. Faction / Special

---

# 5. Survival Branch

## Efficient Hydroponics
- Food output +10%.

## Water Reclamation
- Water output +10%.

## Expanded Battery
- Battery capacity +20%.

## Emergency Rationing
- hunger penalty delayed.

## Medical Sterilization
- Clinic infection recovery improved.

---

# 6. Expedition Branch

## Field Packing
- Supply crafting efficiency.

## Deep Scan
- unlock/strengthen Deep Scout.

## Cargo Harness
- squad Cargo +2.

## Emergency Extraction
- unlock Emergency Beacon recipe/use.

## Route Analysis
- lower bad travel-event chance.

---

# 7. Combat Branch

## Ballistic Calibration
- unlock selected ballistic modifier recipe.

## Energy Focusing
- improve Laser Focus ceiling slightly.

## Blast Control
- unlock improved explosive utility.

## Combat Triage
- improve field medical options.

## Tactical Doctrine
- unlock additional target-priority preset / formation option.

Research should unlock options, not raw universal damage stacking.

---

# 8. Security Branch

## Reinforced Doors
- unlock Door Mk II.

## Sensor Network
- unlock Sensor Node.

## Automated Turret
- unlock basic Sentry Turret.

## Alarm Logic
- modify Alarm threshold/response.

## Response Doctrine
- improve defense squad deployment logic.

---

# 9. Engineering Branch

## Workshop Tooling
- crafting speed.

## Salvage Recovery
- better salvage return.

## Power Management
- custom load-shedding priority.

## Drone Systems
- unlock/upgrade Scout/Defense Drone.

## Prototype Fabrication
- prerequisite for Prototype Forge.

---

# 10. Xenobiology Branch

Unlock around CCL10.

## Alien Tissue Analysis
- better Bestiary/Alien sample use.

## Exposure Monitoring
- show more exact Alien Exposure effects.

## Adaptive Material
- Alien armor recipe family.

## Neural Interface
- Alien utility sidegrade.

## Artifact Theory
- prerequisite for Artifact research.

Every node includes Exposure/risk where appropriate.

---

# 11. Faction / Special Research

Faction unlocks do not sit in one universal tree automatically.

Examples:

Continuity:
- Security Protocol.

Archivists:
- Archive Compression.

Earthborn:
- Surface Conditioning.

Ascendants:
- Hybrid Interface.

These are sidegrades.

# 13. Prerequisite Rule

A research node may require:

- previous node;
- CCL;
- Lab level;
- one discovered source;
- special item.

Avoid trees with 5 irrelevant prerequisites before the desired feature.

Maximum normal prerequisite chain:

```
3 nodes
```

---

# 14. Discovery-Gated Research

Some nodes appear only after discovery.

Examples:

- encounter Mechanical enemy → EMP study;
- recover alien sample → Xenobiology;
- raid Silo 09 → Alarm Logic;
- find old reactor → Reactor Material research.

This connects world exploration to the tech tree.

---

# 15. Research Queue

Uses Research Lab queue:

- L1–2: 1;
- L3–4: 2;
- L5: 3.

Queueing is convenience, not separate currency.

---

# 16. Respec

Permanent research is normally not respeced.

Temporary loadout doctrines may be swapped.

Avoid charging players repeatedly to undo permanent tech choices.

---

# 17. UI

Research screen shows:

- branch;
- node effect;
- prerequisites;
- Research Data cost;
- special source hint;
- time;
- resulting unlock.

Unknown discovery-gated node can show silhouette/??? when useful.

---

# 18. Success Criteria

Research succeeds if:

1. exploration opens technology;
2. choices create new tactics;
3. no giant passive-stat tree;
4. PvE can access complete baseline progression;
5. faction/alien paths create sidegrades and identity.
