# BURROW ZERO — Mimic & Infiltration System v0.1

**Status:** Mid/late-game PvE narrative system

---

# 1. Purpose

Mimic introduces uncertainty inside the safe Silo.

It must create:

- suspicion;
- investigation;
- story;
- risk;

without making random survivor loss feel unfair.

---

# 2. Mimic Forms

## Combat Mimic
Enemy that copies silhouette/behavior.

## Hidden Infiltrator
Entity enters Silo disguised as survivor.

## Mimic Event
False signal / fake distress / copied voice.

These share lore but different mechanics.

---

# 3. First Rule

A normal recruited survivor must never randomly become a Mimic with no warning.

Infiltration originates from identifiable risk sources:

- Alien rescue;
- contaminated expedition;
- unknown Cryo Pod;
- high Alien Exposure;
- suspicious distress signal.

Player gets clues.

---

# 4. Infiltration State

Hidden state:

```
CLEAR
→ SUSPICIOUS
→ INFILTRATED
→ DETECTED
→ CONTAINED / ESCAPED / FOUGHT
```

---

# 5. Suspicion Clues

Examples:

- inconsistent medical scan;
- wrong memory;
- unusual power drain;
- Bio Scanner alert;
- camera anomaly;
- survivor reports strange behavior.

Clues accumulate.

No single clue proves identity.

---

# 6. Investigation

Player may:

- medical scan;
- interview;
- quarantine;
- inspect belongings;
- compare signal data;
- assign security observation.

Costs:
- time;
- Chemicals;
- worker;
- Morale.

---

# 7. False Positive

Investigation can suspect innocent survivor.

But system does not randomly punish with death.

If player quarantines innocent:
- temporary Morale penalty;
- relationship consequence.

This makes suspicion meaningful.

---

# 8. Detection

Detected Mimic may:

- attack;
- flee;
- sabotage;
- reveal lore;
- offer strange interaction in rare branch.

Combat happens in contained room/sector.

---

# 9. Mimic Sabotage

Before detection, possible limited effects:

- minor Power drain;
- contaminate one room;
- steal data;
- disable sensor;
- increase Alien Signal.

No instant destruction or permanent random death.

---

# 10. Counterplay

Helpful systems:

- Bio Scanner;
- Clinic;
- Security;
- high PER;
- Paranoid trait;
- Xenobiology Lab;
- research.

No mandatory single counter.

---

# 11. Combat Mimic

Combat Mimic behavior:

- initially resembles common target/survivor silhouette;
- reveals alien form after damage or scan;
- copies one ability category.

Resistance should not be exact copy.

---

# 12. Fake Distress Signal

World event:

Scout finds survivor signal.

Possible outcomes:
- real survivor;
- ambush;
- Mimic;
- abandoned gear.

Deep Scout increases clue confidence but never guarantees.

---

# 13. Alien Exposure Link

High Alien Exposure:

- increases Mimic/anomaly encounter probability;
- unlocks better alien tech.

This creates risk/reward.

---

# 14. Story Use

Mimic should appear first as curated story chapter before procedural use.

Player learns:
- clues;
- quarantine;
- scan;
- consequences.

Only after tutorial chapter does system enter procedural pool.

---

# 15. Anti-Frustration

Never:

- silently replace favorite survivor;
- permanently kill random survivor offline;
- force impossible detection;
- spam Mimic events.

Frequency:
- rare;
- memorable;
- mostly mid/late-game.

---

# 16. Success Criteria

Mimic works if:

- Silo safety feels occasionally uncertain;
- player can investigate intelligently;
- clues are fair;
- event creates story;
- favorite survivor attachment is not invalidated by RNG.
