# BURROW ZERO — Story Campaign & Chapter Progression v0.1

**Status:** Narrative gameplay baseline  
**Scope:** main PvE campaign structure, not final dialogue script.

---

# 1. Campaign Principle

Story must grow from the same systems the player already uses.

Do not create a separate linear campaign mode.

Story progression should use:

- Silo expansion;
- Radio;
- World Map;
- contracts;
- dungeon expedition;
- NPC Silo raids;
- bosses;
- faction choices;
- Alien research.

---

# 2. Core Mystery

Two centuries after collapse, the player's Silo detects a signal that should not exist.

At first it appears human.

Later evidence suggests:

- multiple Silo networks survived;
- pre-collapse projects knew more than public records admit;
- alien structures may predate the catastrophe;
- some signals may not be human at all.

Central question:

> Are survivors rebuilding Earth, or awakening something already buried beneath it?

---

# 3. Campaign Structure

Prototype main campaign: **8 Chapters**.

Each chapter unlocks systems naturally.

---

# 4. Chapter 0 — Wake

Theme:
Survival.

Player learns:
- Emergency Core;
- Water;
- Food;
- survivors;
- first sector clear.

Story:
Old Silo wakes after long degraded automation.

End beat:

```
INTERNAL SYSTEM:
EXTERNAL ANTENNA CIRCUIT FOUND
STATUS: REPAIRABLE
```

---

# 5. Chapter 1 — First Signal

Theme:
Contact.

Unlocks:
- Radio;
- Scout;
- House/Warehouse targets;
- first rescue.

Story:
A fragmented human distress transmission.

End:
player rescues first outside survivor.

---

# 6. Chapter 2 — The Surface Remembers

Theme:
World identity.

Unlocks:
- Hospital;
- Research Lab;
- Bestiary;
- deeper contracts.

Story:
Evidence of biological anomalies and old response programs.

First major boss:
Hive Mother or equivalent.

---

# 7. Chapter 3 — Silo 09

Theme:
Other bunkers.

Unlocks:
- NPC Silo Raid;
- Alarm;
- hidden layout;
- Military Data.

Story:
A repeating encrypted signal leads to abandoned/hostile Silo 09.

End reveal:
some Silo systems were connected before collapse.

---

# 8. Chapter 4 — Lines in the Dust

Theme:
Human factions.

Unlocks:
- faction reputation;
- faction contracts;
- controlled nodes;
- resource sites.

Story:
Continuity, Free Settlements, Earthborn and Archivists enter the player's map.

Player sees conflicting interpretations of history.

---

# 9. Chapter 5 — The Visitor

Theme:
Alien truth.

Unlocks:
- Alien sites;
- Alien Exposure;
- Xenobiology direction;
- Mimic tutorial.

Story:
An alien crash/structure contains evidence inconsistent with "recent invasion".

Boss:
Fallen Visitor.

End:
ancient material dating conflict.

---

# 10. Chapter 6 — Open Network

Theme:
Human conflict.

Unlocks:
- PvP;
- player Silo signals;
- Combat Data;
- contested resource sites.

Story framing:
the network is reconnecting, but resources are scarce and trust is limited.

PvP is introduced as an option, not mandatory story victory condition.

---

# 11. Chapter 7 — Below Zero

Theme:
Deep Earth.

Unlocks:
- deep Silo sectors;
- Artifact tech;
- Architect-class alien content;
- regional endgame.

Story:
signals originate below known human infrastructure.

Major revelation:
some structures existed before modern civilization.

---

# 12. Chapter 8 — Far Beyond

Theme:
Choice / endgame transition.

Player chooses strategic research direction:

- restore human network;
- hybridize alien technology;
- isolate and defend;
- coalition path.

Choice influences:
- seasonal narrative;
- faction relations;
- tech sidegrades.

No irreversible account lock on first choice; use chapter flags and later reconciliation content.

---

# 13. Story Mission Types

Use existing gameplay:

- rescue;
- recover;
- investigate;
- clear;
- defend;
- infiltrate;
- boss;
- restore;
- scan;
- choose faction.

No dialogue-only mission chain.

---

# 14. Story Rewards

Story gives:

- system unlock;
- survivor;
- unique trait;
- blueprint;
- region;
- faction access;
- cosmetic;
- lore.

Avoid excessive raw resource rewards that trivialize economy.

---

# 15. Lore Delivery

Channels:

- radio;
- terminal;
- survivor memory;
- faction briefing;
- recovered data;
- environmental visual;
- boss sample;
- Silo archive.

Keep text short by default.

Optional deep lore can be expanded separately.

---

# 16. Replay / Branching

Main campaign not replayed from zero.

Branch outcomes stored as flags.

Season/story events can reference:

- faction alignment;
- rescued characters;
- Mimic decisions;
- alien research path.

---

# 17. Success Criteria

Campaign succeeds if:

1. every chapter unlocks gameplay;
2. mystery escalates naturally;
3. PvE alone tells a complete story;
4. PvP appears as world consequence, not narrative requirement;
5. lore does not stop the player from playing.
