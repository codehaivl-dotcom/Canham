# BURROW ZERO — Seasons & Endgame v0.1

**Status:** Long-term content baseline

---

# 1. Endgame Principle

Do not reset the player's Silo.

Persistent progression remains.

Seasonal content changes:

- regions;
- threats;
- story;
- objectives;
- rankings;
- temporary progression layer.

---

# 2. Endgame Pillars

At CCL10–12 player focuses on:

- deep Silo exploration;
- Alien/Artifact research;
- high-tier bosses;
- contested sites;
- factions;
- PvP prestige;
- coalition projects;
- seasonal regions.

---

# 3. Season Length

Prototype:

```
8–12 weeks
```

Long enough for casual players.

Exact duration tested later.

---

# 4. Seasonal Structure

### Week 1–2
Discovery.

### Week 3–5
Escalation.

### Week 6–8
Major threat / boss.

### Final period
resolution + catch-up.

No requirement to login daily.

---

# 5. Season Examples

## Season: First Contact
Alien signals intensify.

## Season: The Hive
Bio threat spreads.

## Season: Architects
Ancient structures activate.

## Season: Surface Return
New surface region opens.

## Season: Signal War
Human Silo network conflict.

---

# 6. Seasonal Region

Can add:

- 1 new region;
- 2–4 new targets;
- enemy variants;
- one boss;
- seasonal resource;
- story chain.

Content should reuse core room/encounter system.

---

# 7. Seasonal Progression

Separate track:

- mission;
- chapter;
- challenge;
- discovery.

Season XP should come from normal play.

Avoid forcing repetitive daily checklist.

---

# 8. Season Pass

If monetized:

Free track:
- gameplay resources;
- cache;
- cosmetics;
- story access.

Paid track:
- cosmetics;
- decoration;
- profile/banner;
- convenience within anti-P2W rules.

No paid-only best weapon/survivor.

---

# 9. Catch-Up

Late joiner:

- accelerated season XP;
- chapter bundles;
- previous weekly objectives remain available.

No "you missed week 2, story gone forever".

---

# 10. Endgame PvE

Endgame PvE includes:

- Extreme expedition;
- rotating boss;
- deep anomaly;
- Artifact hunt;
- faction challenge;
- Silo deep sectors;
- seasonal world event.

PvE alone remains a valid endgame.

---

# 11. Endgame PvP

PvP endgame adds:

- leagues;
- seasonal rating;
- Silo raid modifiers;
- contested sites;
- alliance competition later.

PvP reward primarily:
- prestige;
- cosmetic;
- Combat Data;
- alternate progression.

---

# 12. Artifact Progression

Artifact should be:

- unique mechanic;
- sidegrade/build-defining;
- not simple +50% stats.

Examples:
- shield converts EMP effect;
- weapon changes target behavior;
- alien armor self-heals but raises Exposure.

---

# 13. Coalition Endgame

Alliance/Coalition projects:

- regional radar;
- world boss support;
- temporary defense network;
- research observatory.

Asynchronous contribution.

---

# 14. Season Reset

Reset only:

- seasonal rank;
- seasonal currency;
- temporary region state;
- seasonal objective track.

Do NOT reset:

- Silo;
- survivor;
- permanent gear;
- blueprint;
- Bestiary;
- story history.

---

# 15. Legacy Rewards

Season completion can permanently add:

- cosmetic;
- title;
- room decoration;
- archive entry;
- sidegrade recipe.

Avoid power creep stacking every season.

---

# 16. Endgame Choice

Chapter 8 strategic path can influence seasonal flavor:

- Human Network;
- Alien Hybrid;
- Isolation;
- Coalition.

But all players can access core seasonal content.

---

# 17. Success Criteria

Endgame succeeds if:

1. PvE remains deep;
2. PvP is compelling but optional;
3. no Silo wipe;
4. seasons add novelty without invalidating old gear;
5. returning players can catch up.
